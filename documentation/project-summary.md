# MtaaMart Retail Performance Analysis

## Project Summary

MtaaMart Retail Performance Analysis is an end-to-end retail analytics project built in Microsoft Excel using 8,000 transaction records covering six stores, eight product categories and 30 products.

The project involved data validation, KPI development, store analysis, category analysis, product analysis, customer segmentation, visualization and management recommendations.

## Business Objective

The objective was to understand revenue and profitability performance and identify areas where management could improve product mix, margins, inventory allocation and ongoing performance monitoring.

## Key Results

- Total Revenue: KES 7.06M
- Total Profit: KES 1.57M
- Profit Margin: 22.3%
- Transactions: 8,000
- Units Sold: 23,920

## Key Findings

- Electronics contributed approximately 33.5% of total revenue.
- Food generated KES 1.52M but had the lowest category margin at 14.1%.
- Power Bank generated KES 1.23M and approximately 17.5% of total revenue.
- Thika Road and Nakuru CBD were among the highest-revenue stores.
- Unit volume alone did not explain profitability.

## Recommendations

Management should monitor high-contribution electronics products, review Food category margins, manage inventory concentration, use store-level data to guide allocation and monitor performance regularly.
